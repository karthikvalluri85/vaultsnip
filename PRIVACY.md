# VaultSnip privacy policy

**Short version: VaultSnip stores nothing about you or your images.**

- **No accounts, no backend, no database.** VaultSnip is static code that runs in your browser.
- **Your images stay on your device.** Text recognition (OCR), masking and the safety check run locally in your browser. Images and results are held in memory and disappear when you close the tab.
- **What leaves your device.** Nothing, unless you click *Send and build replica*. Then two things go directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, **Anthropic (Claude)** or **OpenAI**, using your own API key, so that it writes the synthetic text: (1) the *masked* image you approved, and (2) a short list, shown in full in the send dialog under *Show exactly what is sent*, with one entry per hidden box: its kind (number, name, email, ID or label), its position, the visible words next to it that are readable in that image anyway, and either a number's pattern (every digit shown as 9, so "$14.2M" goes as "$99.9M") or, for a name or any other text, only how many characters it had. The hidden text itself never leaves your device. Nothing passes through a VaultSnip server. That provider's API terms and data policy apply to the request. *Build on this device*, *Download masked image* and the samples send nothing at all.
- **Numbers in the masked image are invented.** By default each number (amounts, percentages, scores, temperatures, counts) is replaced with a different, made-up value of the same size: $4.2M might show as $5.6M. The real values never leave your device, but the rough size of each number does (same number of digits, same unit). Names, labels, emails and IDs stay covered by grey boxes, and the image carries a note saying its numbers are made up. Switch it off under *Redaction policy* to cover numbers with grey boxes instead.
- **Customer comments (optional).** If you tick *Keep the wording of comments*, the ordinary words of quotes and free-text feedback stay readable in the image you send, and can appear in the replica and its export. Names, companies, emails, numbers and IDs inside them stay masked, and the replica only quotes words that were readable in the image you approved. It is off by default.
- **Samples** send nothing at all.
- **The replica is your own screenshot, changed in place.** VaultSnip wipes each hidden box with the background around it and draws synthetic text back in the same place, size and colour, in your browser; everything else in the picture stays as it was. The replica therefore shows your dashboard's layout, charts and visible labels exactly as the original does: only the hidden text is replaced. *Build on this device* makes all the synthetic text in your browser (numbers of the same size, made-up names, emails at example.com) and sends nothing; it needs no key. Pointing at a replaced box shows the synthetic value, its kind and the visible words beside it, never the hidden text.
- **What the replica and the masked image still show.** Only text is replaced. Everything drawn stays as in your screenshot: the layout, the visible labels, and the shapes of the charts (bar lengths, line trends, slice sizes, map shading). Numbers are invented but keep their size (the same number of digits and unit), and axis labels are rebuilt on a new scale, yet a chart's proportions are still the real ones. If the shape of a chart is itself confidential, cover the chart with a box on the review screen before you send or share.
- **On-device name check (on by default).** A small AI model looks for safe words used as names ("May Chen", "Delta Airlines") and adds masks for them. It runs in your browser, on this device; the text it reads never leaves it. The model is an app file (19 MB) served by VaultSnip itself, never by a third party; extension packages built for the stores include it. It only adds masks, and you can switch it off under *Redaction policy*.
- **Unread-text check (on by default).** A second small AI model finds every patch of text in the screenshot without reading it, so text the reader missed (tiny, rotated or faint labels) is masked too; a third reads such a patch once more, one line at a time, to tell a safe label from a name. They run in your browser, on this device, and see only the image's pixels; nothing leaves the device. The models are app files (5 MB and 7 MB) served by VaultSnip itself, never by a third party; extension packages built for the stores include them. It only adds masks, and you can switch it off under *Redaction policy*.
- **What your browser keeps.** The OCR language model (an app file, cached so it downloads once) and, only if you tick "remember", your API key, its provider and the model name. You can remove them at any time from "AI key" → "Forget key". "Check key" asks the provider which models the key can use; it sends only the key.
- **Blur text in charts (extension).** "Blur text in charts on this page" (right-click menu, or Alt+Shift+B) reads the charts drawn on that page, finds sensitive text on your device (the same OCR, rules and unread-text check as the app, with the same models packed in the extension), and blurs it in place. Nothing is sent or stored, and running it again removes the blur. It runs only on the tab where you choose it.
- **Extension permissions.** No access to any website until you act on a tab, and none afterwards. `activeTab`: capture or blur the tab you are on, only when you click the icon, the menu item or the shortcut. `scripting`: put the chart blur into that tab when you ask for it. `offscreen`: a hidden extension page that reads the chart text on your device. `contextMenus`: the "Blur text in charts on this page" menu item. None of them shows a warning when you install.
- **Downloads** (the masked PNG, the replica PNG) are made in your browser and saved to your computer; nothing is sent.
- **Safe-to-share receipt.** *Receipt (PNG)* and *Receipt (PDF)* save one page, made in your browser, that lists how many items of each kind were hidden (never the hidden text), the result of the check, the date and time, the VaultSnip version and the SHA-256 fingerprint of the masked image file. It shows a small copy of the masked image, the same picture you approved. Nothing is sent or stored.
- **Clipboard.** VaultSnip reads the clipboard only when you paste (Ctrl+V / ⌘V) or click *Paste a screenshot*, and uses only a picture found there; your browser may ask you to allow it. It writes to the clipboard only when you click *Copy masked image*, *Copy image* (the replica picture, synthetic text only) or *Copy link*. No clipboard permission is added to the extension.
- **Exports.** *Download image* and *Copy image* save or copy the replica picture, made in your browser, with a strip below it saying its text is synthetic. Nothing is sent or stored.
- **Feedback is your choice.** The Feedback button prepares a report (VaultSnip version, browser, the step you were on and any error message, never your screenshot or dashboard text). Nothing is sent until you choose to continue on GitHub, where it is posted under your GitHub account, or to email it from your own mail app. GitHub's or your email provider's terms apply.
- **Developer benchmarks are not part of the app.** The accuracy tools in `tools/` run only when a developer starts them, or in this project's CI. They draw fictional dashboards from invented data, and download a small set of public dashboard images (from Microsoft Learn, Google Cloud documentation, Apache Superset's documentation and Tableau Public) to score against; nothing of yours is involved, and the app and extension never run them. One of them, `tools/ai-bench`, measures the AI-key path: it sends only those invented dashboards, masked, to the AI provider with the developer's own key, read from their environment; the public dashboard images are never sent. The labelling page `tools/layout-bench/annotate.html` runs in the developer's browser and uploads nothing; it keeps the boxes being drawn in that browser's local storage until they are saved as a file.
- **No analytics, cookies or tracking.**
