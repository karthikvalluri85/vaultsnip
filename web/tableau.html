Share a Tableau dashboard screenshot without the data

Tableau dashboards carry their secrets in the marks: names on a bar, amounts in a crosstab, a customer in a tooltip, a region on a map. VaultSnip masks the text on your device and keeps every mark, colour and filter as it was. If a trend itself is the secret, blur the chart in one click.

Open VaultSnip and paste a screenshot

Free and open source. Everything runs in this browser tab: no account, no upload, nothing stored. What stays on your device (everything).

When this helps

  • Asking the Tableau Community or a user group about a calculation or a layout
  • Showing a dashboard design to a prospect built on a current client's data
  • Tableau Public portfolios where the layout is yours and the data is not
  • Screenshots in a runbook, a handover or a ticket

Tip: hide tooltips and filter menus before you snip; what is not on screen cannot leak.

1. PasteTake a screenshot, open VaultSnip, press Ctrl+V. Or drop a PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF.
2. LookMasks appear in seconds; the deeper checks run on and only add masks. Click any box to keep it, drag to add one, blur a chart if its shape is the secret.
3. CopyCopy the masked image into a chat, a ticket or a slide, or make a replica with made-up values in place.

Measured, not promised. On the published test set about 0.25% of the area of sensitive text stayed readable after masking and the check, so take a last look before you share. How it is measured · Also for Power BI, Tableau and Excel screenshots.
