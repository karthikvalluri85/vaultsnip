# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; focus on what is best for the users.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Never publish others' private information, and never post client or personal data in issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the email option in the app (**Feedback → Email instead**). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content, or warn, suspend or ban participants, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
